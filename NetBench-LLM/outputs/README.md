# Evaluation outputs

Per-model answer and judge sheets for the HPN-QA benchmark, under
`by_model/<model>/evaluations/`.

**Read [`DATA_RELEASE.md`](../../DATA_RELEASE.md) first.** Short version:

- Sheets contain **242** questions; the paper scores **233**. The 9-item
  difference was removed by an expert audit (see
  [`analysis/excluded_items.csv`](../../analysis/excluded_items.csv)).
- The `answers/*.xlsx` and `judged/*.xlsx` sheets carry an
  **`excluded_from_scoring`** column (`TRUE` for the 9 audited-out items). Drop
  those rows to get the paper's scored set.
- The in-file `Summary` tabs and the `reports/`/`adaptation_reports/` sheets are
  aggregated over the full 242 and differ slightly from the paper. The
  authoritative 233-scored numbers live in
  [`analysis/outputs/`](../../analysis/outputs/), regenerated by
  `analysis/aggregate_scores.py`.
- Judges: `gpt-5.1` (primary, reported) and `gemini-3.5-flash` (second-judge
  robustness check).
